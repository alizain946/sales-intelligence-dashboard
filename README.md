# 📊 Sales Intelligence Dashboard

An interactive web-based sales analytics dashboard built with **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript** using AI to analyze sales performance, profitability, products, cities, sales representatives, and monthly trends.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Businesses often have sales data spread across different products, cities, sales representatives, and time periods. Manually analyzing this data makes it difficult to identify trends, compare performance, and monitor profitability.

This dashboard provides an interactive way to explore sales data and support **data-driven business decisions**.

## ✨ Key Features

* 📊 Interactive Bar, Line, Pie, and Horizontal Bar charts
* 🔍 Filters for Date, City, Category, and Sales Rep
* 💰 Sales and Profit displayed in USD
* 📈 Monthly Sales Trend
* 🏆 Top 5 / Bottom 5 Products
* 📋 Complete data overview
* 📁 Excel/CSV file upload support

## 📌 KPIs

* **Total Sales**
* **Total Profit**
* **Orders**
* **Average Order Value**

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Focus on high-performing cities and products.
* Optimize discounts to improve profitability.
* Monitor monthly sales trends for better planning.
* Improve sales representative performance through targeted support.
* Review low-performing products and categories.
* Use sales and profit together when making business decisions.
